A small herd with big standards

Mini Highland Co. breeds calm, sturdy miniature Highland cattle for homesteads, hobby farms and families.

Every calf is raised on pasture, handled daily and monitored by our veterinarian. We keep our herd small on purpose, so each animal gets individual attention and every buyer gets a direct line to the people who raised their cow.

We stand behind what we sell: honest descriptions, clear pricing and support long after your calf arrives home.
